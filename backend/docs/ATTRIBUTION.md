# Corpus attribution

The corpus has three kinds of document, listed in the three tables below.

1. **Wikipedia articles**, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Text was
   extracted through the MediaWiki API, stripped of markup, and reformatted so each paragraph carries its article and
   section heading. Only sections relevant to the health and care of dogs and cats were kept. The revision column
   links to the exact MediaWiki revision the text was taken from.
2. **US government publications**, which are in the public domain: consumer pages from the FDA Center for Veterinary
   Medicine, and pages from the CDC's Healthy Pets, Healthy People. Each page's own "content current as of" or
   "reviewed" date is given alongside the date this corpus retrieved it.
3. **Guides written for this project**, covering topics no suitably licensed source covered in the form an owner
   needs. Every claim in them was checked against the sources named in the table. The wording is the project's own,
   and the copyright holder named in the repository's LICENSE licenses the guides under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each guide
   cites its main source in the app. The brand name glossary, the human food guide, the common illnesses guide and
   the owner questions guide instead cite a separate source for every paragraph, named on a SOURCE line in the file.

Common veterinary brand names and what is in them - Are flea and tick chews safe?
Fluralaner, afoxolaner, sarolaner and lotilaner all belong to the isoxazoline class. The US Food and Drug Administration has alerted owners and veterinarians to the potential for neurological side effects in dogs and cats treated with this class, while noting that these products can be and have been used safely in the majority of animals, and it advises going through the animal's medical history with a veterinarian before choosing one.
SOURCE: Fact Sheet about Potential Adverse Events Associated with Isoxazoline Flea and Tick Products (FDA) | https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/animal-health-literacy/fact-sheet-pet-owners-and-veterinarians-about-potential-adverse-events-associated-isoxazoline-flea

Common veterinary brand names and what is in them - IVDD, intervertebral disc disease of the spine
IVDD stands for intervertebral disc disease, also spelled disk. Signs usually appear after about five years of age and consist of a slowly progressive unsteady, weak gait. Mild cases are managed without surgery, while animals with severe neurological signs and spinal cord compression do best with prompt surgery.
